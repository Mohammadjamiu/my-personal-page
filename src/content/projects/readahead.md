# ReadAhead

Personal experiment in **fast, low-friction reading**: combine PDF handling, lightweight account storage for people who want a cloud copy, and **IndexedDB** for a no-upload path. Built for readers who want structure (summaries, continuity, forward hints) without giving up privacy by default.
