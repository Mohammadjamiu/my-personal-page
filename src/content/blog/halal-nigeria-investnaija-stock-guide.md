## What is InvestNaija?

**InvestNaija** is a Nigerian brokerage app for buying and selling **NGX-listed** stocks from your phone.

Compared with **Lotus Capital** or **One17 Capital** (more mutual-fund / portfolio style), InvestNaija is **direct single-stock** access — which means **more responsibility** on you to pick and screen names.

**Links**

- [investnaija.com](https://investnaija.com/)  
- [Android (Play Store search)](https://play.google.com/store/search?q=investnaija&c=apps)  
- [iOS App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ng/app/investnaija/id1579193940)

## 1. Download and sign up (KYC)

1. Install the app from the stores above.  
2. Complete **KYC** — typically BVN, bank account, NIN, address, next of kin, **CSCS** number (needed for NGX settlement), security questions, and a risk questionnaire.

> **CSCS** = Central Securities Clearing System — standard for Nigerian equities.

![KYC or signup step](/blog/halal-nigeria-investnaija/01.jpg)

3. After approval you should see the main tabs such as **Home**, **LearnIN**, **SaveIN**, **InvestIN**, **Profile**.

## 2. Fund your trading account

1. Open **InvestIN**.  

![InvestIN home cards](/blog/halal-nigeria-investnaija/02.jpg)

You should see cards similar to **NGN trading balance**, **View portfolio**, and **Explore securities**.

![InvestIN secondary card area](/blog/halal-nigeria-investnaija/03.jpg)

2. Tap **View portfolio** on the first card.  
3. Tap **Fund account**.  

![Fund account entry](/blog/halal-nigeria-investnaija/04.jpg)

4. Enter an amount (example: ₦2,000).  

![Funding amount](/blog/halal-nigeria-investnaija/05.jpg)

5. Choose **Paystack** → **Other payment methods** as the flow allows.  

![Paystack path](/blog/halal-nigeria-investnaija/06.jpg)

![Payment method options](/blog/halal-nigeria-investnaija/07.jpg)

![Paystack alternatives](/blog/halal-nigeria-investnaija/08.jpg)

6. Select **Pay via transfer** when offered.  

![Virtual account transfer screen](/blog/halal-nigeria-investnaija/09.jpg)

You should see bank name, **virtual account number**, and amount.

![Transfer instructions detail](/blog/halal-nigeria-investnaija/10.jpg)

7. After you send the transfer, tap **I’ve sent the money** (or the equivalent confirmation).  

![Confirmation after transfer](/blog/halal-nigeria-investnaija/11.jpg)

When it clears, your **NGN trading balance** should update.

## 3. Buy a stock (example flow)

> The notes I wrote used **Dangote Sugar** only as a **UI example**. In the [Nigeria halal stock tracker](/articles/halal-nigeria-stock-tracker) snapshot, that name **failed** a common debt screen — use this section to learn **where the buttons are**, not as a recommendation to buy any specific ticker.

1. From **InvestIN**, move to the **portfolio** card (often the second card in the row).  

![Portfolio card swipe](/blog/halal-nigeria-investnaija/12.jpg)

2. Tap **View portfolio** → **Start investing**.  

![Start investing](/blog/halal-nigeria-investnaija/13.jpg)

3. Browse the issuer list.  

![Listed companies list](/blog/halal-nigeria-investnaija/14.jpg)

4. Tap a company to open its quote screen.  

![Stock quote overview](/blog/halal-nigeria-investnaija/15.jpg)

5. Inspect chart range if useful.  

![Price chart](/blog/halal-nigeria-investnaija/16.jpg)

6. Tap **Buy** when you are ready.  

![Buy button](/blog/halal-nigeria-investnaija/17.jpg)

7. Choose **order type** (market vs limit) and time-in-force (e.g. good-for-day / good-till-cancelled).  

![Order type and TIF](/blog/halal-nigeria-investnaija/18.jpg)

![Order form continuation](/blog/halal-nigeria-investnaija/19.jpg)

![Units and limits](/blog/halal-nigeria-investnaija/20.jpg)

8. Enter **units** (example: 20).  

![Units field](/blog/halal-nigeria-investnaija/21.jpg)

9. Review **estimated total and fees**.  

![Fees and total](/blog/halal-nigeria-investnaija/22.jpg)

> **Brokerage and exchange fees are not riba** in the usual sense — they are service and regulatory costs. Still read the breakdown so you know what you pay.

10. Submit **Buy stock**. On success you should see **Executed** (or similar) and receive confirmations / contract notes.  

![Executed order](/blog/halal-nigeria-investnaija/23.jpg)

## Common questions

**Q: Are InvestNaija fees riba?**  
**A:** They are operational and market charges, not “guaranteed return on a loan” riba — but **read** the line items.

**Q: Are all stocks halal?**  
**A:** No. You must screen business lines and **financial ratios** (debt, impure income, etc.).
